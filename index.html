About

My work so far has run along three threads.

LLM safety. My undergraduate thesis with Dr. Amitabha Chakrabarty introduced Dynamic Gated Distillation, a dual-teacher method that makes small language models harder to jailbreak without hurting their reasoning. Alongside it, I benchmarked how different LLMs hold up against jailbreak prompts, which became my ICECTE 2026 paper.

Multimodal learning. At AMIR Lab I designed MGQ-Former, a tri-modal fusion model for remote sensing VQA that keeps fine-grained regional detail and reached 78.3% accuracy on EarthVQA.

AI for genomics. I fine-tuned the Omni-DNA language model for promoter and enhancer classification (over 90% accuracy), and I'm now building that into CisRAG, an agentic RAG system that reasons directly over raw DNA sequences.

Research Focus

01

LLM Safety & Jailbreak

How LLMs fail under jailbreak prompts, and post-training methods like knowledge distillation that make smaller models robust while keeping their reasoning intact.

02

Agentic AI & RAG

Retrieval systems that route each query to the right source based on what that source can actually do, with reasoning that resists hallucination.

03

Multimodal Learning

Vision-language fusion and Q-Former style architectures for visual question answering, especially where fine-grained regional detail matters.

04

AI for Genomics

DNA language models for promoter and enhancer classification, and agents that reason over raw genomic sequences.
