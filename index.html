Research Focus

My research interests lie in machine learning, NLP, and large language models, with a focus on making them safer and more useful in specialized domains:

LLM Safety & Jailbreak
Benchmarking LLMs against jailbreak prompts, and post-training methods like dual-teacher knowledge distillation that make small models more robust without hurting their reasoning.

Agentic AI & RAG
Agentic retrieval systems that decide which source to trust, with capability-aware routing and hallucination-resistant reasoning, applied to raw DNA sequence prompts.

Multimodal Learning
Vision-language fusion and Q-Former based architectures for remote sensing visual question answering.

Genomic Language Models
Fine-tuning DNA language models for promoter and enhancer classification.

Research

What I have built so far, across LLM safety, agentic AI, and multimodal learning.

01

CisRAG

BRAC University · Dr. Swakkhar Shatabda · Sep 2026 – Present

A novel dynamic routing algorithm for a bio-agentic RAG system in genomics. It picks the right knowledge source based on what each source can actually do, and reasons over raw DNA sequence prompts while resisting hallucination.

02

MGQ-Former

AMIR Lab · Dr. M. Firoz Mridha · Feb 2026 – Aug 2026

A tri-modal fusion architecture for remote sensing VQA. It combines vision-language representations with a dual-path gated pooling mechanism that keeps fine-grained regional details, reaching 78.3% accuracy on EarthVQA.

03

Dynamic Gated Distillation

Undergraduate Thesis · Dr. Amitabha Chakrabarty · 2025 – 2026

A dual-teacher framework for jailbreak prevention in LLMs. A harm-score predictor decides how to blend two teachers during distillation, so smaller models become more robust to jailbreaks while keeping their general reasoning.

04

Omni-DNA Fine-tuning

Genomic Sequence Classification

Fine-tuned the Omni-DNA genomic LLM on human promoter and enhancer data. Training on the merged dataset avoided overfitting to a single task and reached over 90% accuracy.
