Research

Selected research

My work spans language model safety, multimodal AI, and biological sequence modeling. These projects explore different aspects of building more reliable AI systems.

01 · LLM SAFETY

Dynamic Gated Knowledge Distillation

A dual-teacher knowledge distillation framework designed to reduce harmful capability transfer while preserving general reasoning ability in smaller language models.

02 · GENOMICS + RAG

CisRAG

A confidence-routed agentic retrieval framework for promoter and enhancer reasoning from raw DNA sequences, combining sequence classification, regulatory knowledge retrieval, and motif analysis.

03 · MULTIMODAL AI

Reliable Visual Question Answering

Research on multimodal question answering with an emphasis on uncertainty estimation, regional reasoning, and reliable prediction in specialized visual domains.

04 · VISION-LANGUAGE MODELS

Multimodal Fusion for VQA

Exploration of transformer-based vision-language architectures for relational and domain-specific visual question answering.
